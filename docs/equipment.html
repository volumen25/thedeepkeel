Fender Blues Jr III

The Fender Blues Junior III is a member of Fender’s renowned Hot Rod amplifier series, found on stages around the world. Known for its unmistakable Fender tone, the Blues Junior III is a 15-watt all-tube combo amplifier that serves as an excellent platform for pedals while delivering warm clean tones and rich tube overdrive.

A longtime favourite for both stage and studio, it features EL84 power tubes, 12AX7 preamp tubes, genuine spring reverb, a simple control layout, a footswitchable FAT boost, and support for an external speaker cabinet.

Fender Jaguar

Respecting their heritage while offering a modern twist on a classic, Fender’s Player Series Jaguar updates the popular short-scale instrument with modern features and pickups. Starting with the surf, indie rock and punk favourite’s iconic offset body and easy-playing 24” scale length, Fender refines the design with freshly designed Player Series pickups, for an exceptional range of available tones. The maple neck now boasts a contemporary 9.5” radius pau ferro fingerboard, and the Adjusto-matic bridge and anchored tailpiece offers stable tuning and rock-solid intonation. Always a guitar for the musically adventurous, the Player Series Jaguar is ready for you to create whatever music floats into your head.

JOYO JF-09 TREMOLO

The JOYO JF-09 Tremolo recreates the classic tremolo effect found in vintage tube amplifiers. It uses the same photoelectric tube circuitry found in many classic amp designs, producing a warm, smooth, and musical modulation. Simple Intensity and Rate controls make it easy to dial in anything from a subtle shimmer to a deep pulsing tremolo.

The pedal features true bypass switching to minimize tone loss when disengaged and is housed in a durable aluminum alloy enclosure with a stoving varnish finish.

Features

  • Vintage tube amplifier-style tremolo
  • Photoelectric tube circuit emulation
  • Warm, smooth modulation
  • Simple two-knob operation
  • True bypass switching
  • Rugged aluminum alloy enclosure
  • Durable stoving varnish finish
